# Navigator surface brief

## THESIS

The public value is orientation: one calm, trustworthy place to find Singapore AI organizations and understand how each entry reached the index.

## OWN-WORLD

Extend the established Superintelligence SG world: midnight navy, warm white, Singapore gold, editorial rules, geometric signal mark and direct language.

## STORY

Begin with search, reveal the ecosystem as a navigable public index, distinguish public references from self-submitted profiles, then invite organizations to publish their own presence.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

Show the proposition, search control and source model in one viewport. The primary action opens the Navigator; Lab and Commons remain visible as supporting routes.

## FORM

Use a full-width editorial list, thin rules, compact source labels and generous typographic hierarchy. Avoid generic feature cards, decorative statistics and unsupported completeness claims.

## FINISH

unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
