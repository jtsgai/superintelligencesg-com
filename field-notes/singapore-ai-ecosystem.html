A map is a starting point, not a verdict.

Singapore's AI ecosystem is easier to enter when public programmes, research groups and assurance work can be seen together. This page gathers a few reliable starting points and leaves room for the organisations we have not found yet.

Four ways in.

There is no single centre of AI work. Some teams build capability, some ask foundational questions, some help organisations move from experiment to deployment, and some work on testing and assurance.

Use the cards as doors into the public record. Then visit the Commons to add an organisation's own perspective.

How to read the map.

Self-submitted does not mean verified.
Superintelligence SG's Commons is an open directory. Organisations describe their own work, and listings can be corrected or removed by email. The directory is designed to make discovery easier, not to confer endorsement.

When you compare programmes or organisations, ask: What problem is being addressed? Who can use the result? What evidence is public? Who is responsible when the system is wrong?

For a hands-on version of those questions, open the Exploration Lab.
