# Superintelligence SG design system

## Design intent

Superintelligence SG should feel like a public institution from the near future: calm, useful, open and precise. The interface gives visitors orientation before asking for participation.

## Visual system

- **Surface:** midnight navy `#0b1521`
- **Raised surface:** deep blue `#112335`
- **Primary text:** warm white `#f4f0e7`
- **Secondary text:** blue grey `#b5c1c9`
- **Rules:** slate `#314758`
- **Accent:** Singapore gold `#f6c75b`
- **Typography:** Avenir Next with system fallbacks
- **Shape:** restrained 14px radii for controls; editorial rows use rules and spacing

## Hierarchy

Display headlines are large, tightly spaced and limited to one highlighted idea. Body copy stays narrow enough for comfortable reading. Gold identifies actions, source labels and the active route.

## Components

- The global header always exposes Navigator, Lab and Commons.
- Search is the primary control on the `.com` homepage and Navigator.
- Directory results are editorial rows rather than a grid of cards.
- Each result shows its source status and its next action.
- Public references use letter marks; community profiles use the organization logo supplied through the Commons.

## Content rules

- Do not describe the index as complete, official, certified or ranked.
- Use **Public reference** for editorial starting points linked to public sources.
- Use **Community profile** and **Self-submitted** for profiles published by organizations.
- Keep calls to action concrete: search, visit, explore or publish.

## Motion and accessibility

Motion is limited to hover and focus feedback. Reduced motion preferences are respected. Focus rings use the gold accent. Layouts collapse to a single reading column on small screens without horizontal overflow.

## Raster provenance

- `assets/com-hero-v2.jpg`: existing Superintelligence SG project artwork from the approved full-bleed homepage direction; no external stock source is used in this build.
- `assets/singapore-ai-ecosystem-og.png`: existing social preview artwork created for the Singapore AI Ecosystem Map within this project.
- Organization logos in the Navigator are supplied by the organizations through the Commons and are displayed from the published profile data.

## Finish review

Reviewed on 28 September 2026 at desktop and 390px mobile widths. The first viewport communicates the product, search is usable, result source labels are explicit and no horizontal overflow was found. The active environment did not permit a separate reviewer agent, so the review was completed in-thread against the surface brief and craft floor.

**Verdict:** approved to ship.
