# Superintelligence SG

## Product purpose

Superintelligence SG helps people find, understand and join Singapore's AI ecosystem. The product connects three domains with distinct jobs:

- `superintelligencesg.com` is the public navigator and main entry point.
- `superintelligencesg.org` is the open commons where organizations publish their own profiles without approval.
- `superintelligencesg.ai` is the lab for questions, research and exploration.

## Primary audiences

- People looking for Singapore AI organizations, programmes and research.
- Organizations that want to make their work discoverable.
- Researchers, builders and public-interest participants looking for local context.

## Core promise

Find the Singapore AI ecosystem in one clear place, then follow the organizations and ideas that matter to you.

## Trust model

The navigator distinguishes two source types:

- **Public references** are a small editorial starting index linked to public sources. They are not endorsements, rankings or complete coverage.
- **Community profiles** are published directly through the Commons by the organizations they represent.

The site does not imply certification, membership approval or official government status. Every route explains its source and offers a correction path.

## Experience principles

1. Search and orientation appear before brand explanation.
2. Source status stays visible at result level.
3. Organizations choose whether to publish a community profile.
4. The three domains guide visitors to one another without duplicating their jobs.
5. Language stays practical, public-facing and free of unsupported claims.

## Current scope

- A unified navigator on `.com` combining a small public starting index with live self-submitted Commons profiles.
- Search and filters for source type and area of work.
- A clear route to publish an organization on `.org`.
- Cross-domain navigation between Navigator, Lab and Commons.
